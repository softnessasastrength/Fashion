# **1. Project Overview**

Starlink One is a signature engagement/statement ring in the Renn design system: a heart-shaped natural emerald center stone in a secure 4-prong setting, flanked by a chain-link / infinity motif on both shoulders, executed in full 925 sterling silver with an initial high-polish finish that is intentionally left unprotected to allow natural tarnish and patina to develop with wear.

### **1.1 Design Intent**

Center stone reads as the visual anchor: vivid green, brilliant-faceted heart, set high enough to catch light from the side without being fragile.

Chain-link/infinity motif on the shoulders is the signature element of the piece — it must be treated as a precision-matched pair (mirrored left/right), not a generic decorative filler.

Finish is deliberately "alive": no rhodium, no anti-tarnish lacquer. The metal is expected to oxidize and patina over time — this is a design feature, not a defect, and must be communicated to the factory so it is not treated as a QC failure.

### **1.2 Governing Source of Truth**

Four client reference images define the exact chain-link/infinity geometry and are the single source of truth for that motif. This package was generated before those images were available in this session — Section 6 (Chain-Link / Infinity Motif Specification) is structured as a fill-in template pending upload, and CAD/manufacturing work on the motif should not proceed to tooling until Section 6 is finalized against the reference images.

### **4.1 Durability Note (flag for client awareness)**

Emerald is significantly softer and more brittle than diamond or sapphire (Mohs 7.5–8 but with high inclusion density and low toughness), and heart shapes concentrate stress at two points: the center cleft (V-notch) and the bottom tip. This design's prong layout (Section 5) places a prong at both of those exact points deliberately, and the piece should be marketed/cared-for as a stone requiring the same care as other emerald jewelry (avoid impact, avoid ultrasonic cleaning, avoid steam cleaning).

# **5. Setting & Prong Specification**

### **5.1 Prong Layout (4-prong, heart-specific placement)**

Prong 1 — Point prong: centered on the bottom tip (V-point) of the heart. This is the single most vulnerable point on the stone and must fully cradle the tip, not just touch it.

Prong 2 — Cleft prong: centered on the top cleft (V-notch). Recommend a split/notched "V-prong" tip profile so the prong hugs both facet planes of the cleft rather than resting on a single point.

Prong 3 & 4 — Shoulder prongs: positioned at the widest point of each heart lobe (left and right rounded shoulders of the heart), symmetric about the centerline.

### **5.2 Prong Construction**

| Feature | Spec |

| --- | --- |

| Prong profile | Round, tapering claw — no faceted/square prongs |

| Base diameter | 1.2 mm (±0.05 mm) |

| Tip diameter after finishing | 0.8 mm (±0.05 mm) |

| Tip shape | Rounded bead tip (snag-free) — cleft prong (Prong 2) is split/notched per 5.1 |

| Metal coverage over crown/girdle | 0.6–0.8 mm per prong, enough to lock the girdle without covering table-facing facets |

| Prong-to-gallery attachment | Cast integral to basket — no soldered-on prongs |



### **5.3 Gallery / Basket**

Open 4-rail basket (one rail feeding each prong) to allow light transmission through the pavilion — supports "brilliant cut" visual intent.

Basket wire gauge: 1.0 mm round, matched to shank metal for uniform patina.

Seat cut to the exact pavilion angle of the approved heart-brilliant stone — seat must be re-cut in CAD after the physical stone's actual depth % is confirmed (Section 4).

### **6.2 Structural / Manufacturing Decision Required**

Two fundamentally different builds are possible and must be decided before CAD starts, since they change tolerances, printing method, and cost:

Option A — Cast solid motif (recommended default): links are modeled and cast as one fused piece with the shank/basket. Visually reads as chain-link but does not move. Stronger, cheaper, standard for this kind of ring.

Option B — Articulated/functional links: links are cast or fabricated separately and mechanically joined so they move. Adds significant cost, fragility, and assembly steps; typically reserved for pieces where the chain motion itself is the point (e.g., a bracelet).

Recommendation: Option A, unless the reference images clearly show the links must swing/rotate independently — flag to client for explicit sign-off either way.

# **9. Manufacturing Brief — English**

### **Project**

Starlink One — heart-cut emerald ring, 925 sterling silver, chain-link/infinity shoulder motif. Client: Renn / Chaos Incorporated.

### **To the Factory**

Please read this brief in full before quoting or starting CAD. This piece has two non-negotiable requirements that differ from a standard ring order: (1) the finish must NOT be rhodium-plated or lacquer-coated — the metal is meant to tarnish naturally over time, and (2) the shoulder chain-link motif must match the client's four reference images exactly in link count, shape, and proportion, not a generic "chain pattern." Reference images will be supplied separately/appended; if they are missing from your packet, stop and request them before modeling.

### **Materials**

Metal: 925 sterling silver throughout (shank, gallery, prongs, and motif) — no base-metal core, no plating.

Center stone: natural heart-shaped brilliant-cut emerald, vivid green, 8–9 mm, supplied with independent gemological certification (GIA/AGL or equivalent). Stone will be supplied by client/sourcing partner unless otherwise agreed — confirm supply chain before quoting stone-setting labor only vs. stone + setting.

### **Construction**

Ring size US 7 — finished inner diameter 17.3 mm ±0.1 mm, measured after casting, polishing, and stone-setting (not before).

4-prong setting, prong placement locked to the heart's point, cleft, and two shoulders (see Section 5 of the full tech pack) — this is not a generic 4-prong layout and must not be substituted with an evenly-spaced 4-prong pattern.

Chain-link/infinity motif cast integral to the piece (no soldered-on links) unless the reference images explicitly call for articulated/moving links — if in doubt, ask before committing to tooling.

### **Finish**

High polish / mirror finish on all surfaces, including motif undercuts — no matte or satin zones.

No rhodium plating. No anti-tarnish coating, lacquer, or e-coat of any kind.

"925" hallmark plus Chaos Incorporated maker's mark stamped on interior shank, legible at 10x, stamped before final polish.

### **Handling**

After stone-setting: hand-clean only. No ultrasonic or steam cleaning once the emerald is in place (oil-treated emerald can be damaged by both).

Ship with light protective wrap only — do not include anti-tarnish paper/strips in packaging, as this works against the intended patina.

### **Approval Gate**

A resin/wax 3D print of the full CAD model (stone-in-place, to scale) must be sent for client sign-off before metal casting begins. Do not proceed to casting on CAD approval alone.

### **项目名称**

Starlink One（星链一号）—— 心形祖母绿戒指，925纯银材质，双肩链条/无限符号纹样。客户：Renn / Chaos Incorporated（混沌股份）。

### **致工厂说明**

请在报价或开始建模前完整阅读本说明书。本产品有两项与常规订单不同的强制性要求，不可协商：（1）成品表面禁止镀铑、禁止上光油或任何防氧化涂层——银材需要随佩戴自然氧化、产生包浆，这是设计效果的一部分；（2）戒指双肩的链条/无限符号纹样必须与客户提供的四张参考图片完全一致（包括链节数量、形状与比例），不可使用通用链条纹样替代。参考图片将单独提供或随附件发送；若您收到的资料中缺少参考图片，请在建模前暂停并索取，切勿自行推测纹样设计。

### **材质要求**

金属材质：戒圈、爪镶篮、爪齿及链条纹样全部使用925纯银（含银量92.5%），不得使用贱金属芯材，成品不得电镀。

主石：天然心形明亮式切割祖母绿，鲜绿色，尺寸8–9毫米，须附独立宝石鉴定证书（GIA、AGL或同等资质机构出具）。除另有约定外，主石由客户/寻石方提供——报价前请与客户确认是仅报镶嵌工费，还是包含主石采购。

### **结构与尺寸**

戒指尺寸：美码7号——成品内径17.3毫米，公差±0.1毫米，须在铸造、抛光及镶石全部完成后测量，而非铸造前的蜡型尺寸。

镶嵌方式：四爪镶嵌，爪位须严格对应心形宝石的底部尖端、顶部凹口（心形缺口）及左右两侧肩部（详见完整技术包第5节），此为针对心形宝石的专用爪位设计，不可替换为常规四爪均匀分布方案。

链条/无限符号纹样须与戒身、爪镶篮一体铸造成型，不允许后期焊接拼装链节；若参考图片明确要求链节可活动（真实链条结构），须在开模前与客户书面确认，不可自行判断。

### **表面处理**

全器表面（含纹样凹陷处）均须抛光至镜面高抛光效果，不得留有哑光或磨砂区域。

禁止镀铑，禁止使用任何防氧化涂层、封层或电镀处理。

须在戒圈内侧打印“925”纯度印记及Chaos Incorporated品牌印记，印记须在10倍放大镜下清晰可辨，并在最终抛光前完成打印，以确保印记边缘整洁。

### **镶石后处理规范**

主石镶嵌完成后，仅可手工清洁，严禁使用超声波或蒸汽清洗——祖母绿通常经过注油处理，超声波及蒸汽清洗均可能对宝石造成损伤（裂纹扩大或注油流失）。

包装时仅可使用轻质保护包裹材料，禁止在包装内放置防氧化纸或防氧化条，因其效果与本设计要求的自然包浆效果相悖。

### **确认节点（重要）**

金属铸造前，须先提供按实际比例、含仿真主石的树脂或蜡质3D打印样品，交由客户确认签字后方可进入铸造环节。仅凭CAD电子图纸批准不可直接进入铸造工序。

### **11.2 Modeling Requirements**

Model the stone as an accurate proxy (correct heart-brilliant facet geometry at 8–9 mm) so the seat/prongs are engineered against real geometry, not a placeholder sphere/box.

Chain-link/infinity motif must be modeled from the four reference images at 1:1 scale reference overlay — do not approximate by eye.

Minimum wall thickness anywhere in the cast structure: 0.8 mm (castability floor for 925 silver investment casting).

All undercuts in the motif must include adequate draft/relief for investment mold release — flag any link geometry that fully encloses a void (true interlocking rings) since those cannot be cast as one piece and require the Section 6.2 articulated-link decision.

Model at the exact target ring size (17.3 mm ID) — do not rely on post-cast sizing to hit tolerance, since sizing after the fact can distort the motif.
